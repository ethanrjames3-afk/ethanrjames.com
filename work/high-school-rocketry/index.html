High school rocketry

Rocket Builder · NASA Student Launch and TARC

In high school I competed in two rocketry programs.

  • NASA Student Launch: as a freshman, I was the only one on the team who had built rockets before, so I built our entire rocket. I taught myself how from YouTube videos, building on what I’d learned flying model rockets.
  • Team America Rocketry Challenge (TARC): the clip above is one of our TARC rockets leaving the rail.

It came full circle in 2020, when my Vanderbilt team won NASA Student Launch: see the NASA Student Launch project.
