Tidewing

Designer and Builder · Autonomous boat, in progress

Tidewing is a car-portable autonomous boat for San Francisco Bay that I’m designing and building.

  • The boat: a trimaran, with a main hull and two floats joined by carbon fiber beams. The hulls are 3D-printed and sheathed in fiberglass and epoxy.
  • The brains: ArduPilot steers it, using flying-wing RC gear and a flight controller with GPS.
  • The current build drives on an underwater thruster. It’s a quiet, efficient testbed for a long-endurance solar-electric version.
  • Next: hydrofoils with active ride-height control, and a free-rotating wingsail.
