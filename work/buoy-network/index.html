Buoy mesh network

Mechanical Engineer Intern · Booz Allen Hamilton Summer Games

During my 2018 internship at Booz Allen Hamilton, our team built a mesh network of buoys for pattern-of-life monitoring.

  • I was the team’s mechanical engineer. I built a custom electronics enclosure for the buoy that integrated with the launcher.
  • I selected the sensor suite and sized the battery to run for 24 hours, then scuttle.
  • I ran a series of tests to verify the buoy worked.
