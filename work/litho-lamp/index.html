Litho-Lamp

Designer and Developer · litho-lamp.com

Litho-Lamp is a free web app I built that turns photos into 3D-printable lithophane lamp shades. A lithophane is a thin print whose thickness follows the photo, so the image appears when light shines through it.

  • Upload a photo, or a different one for each of four sides, and preview the shade in 3D, lit or solid.
  • Choose a shade shape: drum, empire, coolie, barrel, square and more. Then set the size, wall thickness and resolution.
  • Add a mounting ring for a standard E26/E27 lamp socket, and see an estimate of the PLA cost.
  • Export an STL ready to print. Everything runs in the browser.
