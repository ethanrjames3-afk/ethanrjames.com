I'm designing airplanes whose airframes are kits of flat, laser-cut aluminum parts ordered from OSH Cut, plus standard extrusions, fasteners and off-the-shelf parts. Both are designs checked by analysis only, so far. Neither has been built or flown.

**OSH-2** is a two-seat, side-by-side, strut-braced high-wing plane, designed as an Experimental Amateur-Built aircraft under the MOSAIC light-sport rules so a sport pilot can fly it. It has a Rotax 915 iS engine and a constant-speed propeller, and every sheet metal part is laser-cut and, where needed, press-braked by OSH Cut. Design estimates put it at about 100 knots in cruise with roughly 300 nautical miles of range.

**AJ-2 Tinwing** is an all-metal, riveted two-seat sailplane with a retractable turbojet. The jet lets it take off on its own or keep it up when the lift runs out, so you can fly home instead of landing in a field. The airframe is a kit of laser-cut flat parts from OSH Cut, with a 17 m wing and a best glide ratio of about 32 to 1. It's in design review now.
