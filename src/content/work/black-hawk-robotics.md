I was on Black Hawk Robotics in 2015–16, doing drive base CAD. I came back as an engineering mentor.

I led the design of the skills robot's drive base with a team of four students. It's optimized for an at-home driving obstacle course, with high maneuverability and a high power-to-weight ratio. We compared an off-the-shelf swerve module with one we designed ourselves.
