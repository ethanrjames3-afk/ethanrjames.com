During my 2019 internship at Booz Allen Hamilton, our team built a distributed network of cameras for object recognition and person tracking.

- I was the team's mechanical engineer. I built a custom enclosure for the camera, processor and supporting electronics.
- I designed and built the front end of our Android app, which connects the user to the base station.
- I managed the system requirements and verified compliance.
