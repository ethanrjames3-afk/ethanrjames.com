From October 2020 to September 2021 I worked full time at NASA Langley Research Center as a contractor through Booz Allen Hamilton.

I used model-based systems engineering (MBSE) principles and tools to model how the NASA Atmospheric Science Data Center ingests, archives and distributes atmospheric science data, documenting its data processing workflows and metadata requirements.
