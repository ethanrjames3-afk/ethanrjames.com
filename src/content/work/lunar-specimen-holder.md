My senior design project with NASA Marshall Space Flight Center: a sample holder to study the long-term effects of the lunar environment. It was designed to interface with a commercial lander.

- I was primarily responsible for the CAD, in Onshape and SolidWorks, and for all of the manufacturing.
- I ran FEA on the critical parts for the deployment stage of the mission.
- I wrote the documentation proving the design worked, for our NASA materials engineer advisor.
