I was a field engineer for GE Aviation at Southwest Airlines' maintenance headquarters.

- Monitored engine data to maximize engine performance.
- Managed the logistics of engine maintenance.
- Helped make sure Southwest was ready for the introduction of the LEAP-1B engine.
- Monitored a static fire to confirm an engine was within operational limits.
- Automated one of my own tasks in MATLAB to make it more efficient.
